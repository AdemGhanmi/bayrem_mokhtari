THE STORY.

A long football journey: coaching environments, first-team experience, youth football and years of analysis across regional media.

1981–2007

Born in El Kef

Bayrem Mokhtari was born in 1981 in El Kef, in north-western Tunisia. He trained at ISSEP Kef, the region's sports institute, and chose coaching early.

His first professional chapter, from 2005 to 2007, was at Olympique du Kef, the club of his home city.

2007–2009

Tunis and the youth teams

In 2007 he joined Espérance Sportive de Tunis to coach the Olympic squad, a demanding environment where results are expected every week. The following season he worked inside Tunisia's youth national setup, focused on developing the next generation of players.

2009–2013

Saudi Arabia: the breakthrough years

In 2009 he moved to Saudi Arabia and took charge of Al Jeel in the First Division after the departure of compatriot Lotfi Kadri, whom he had assisted. From 2010 to 2013 he was assistant coach at Al Fateh, a period that included the Saudi league title and the first Saudi Super Cup in the club's history.

2013–2017

Egypt and Al Ittihad

After a season at Smouha in the Egyptian Premier League (2013–2014), he joined Al Ittihad in Jeddah as assistant coach. He led the 2015 pre-season preparation and became popular with the fans, who nicknamed him “the loyal”. He returned to the club in 2016–2017.

2015–2021

Head coach in three countries

In 2015–2016 he took his first head-coach role in the Qatar Stars League with Al Shamal. He then coached Damac in Saudi Arabia (2017–2018), Al Orouba in Oman (2018–2019) and Al Orobah in Saudi Arabia (2019–2021), where the board renewed his contract for a second season.

Today

Analyst, writer, founder

Alongside the touchline, he has worked as a football analyst for outlets including Nessma, M Tunisia, Tunisia's national radio and Mix FM Saudi Arabia, and writes about the game.

He also leads Elite Sports Innovation, the company behind the Pédagogue Sportif platform for coaches, and works as a trainer and speaker.
